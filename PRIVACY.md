# Zyro Discord Bot — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Zyro** ("Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the Bot within Discord servers. We are committed to transparency and respecting your privacy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
Zyro collects only the minimal data strictly necessary to operate its features:

- **Discord Identifiers**:
  - User IDs, Guild (Server) IDs, Channel IDs, and Role IDs to link settings, permissions, levels, tickets, and invites to the correct entities.
- **Server Configuration Data**:
  - Channel configurations (e.g., log channels, ticket categories, welcome channels, autoroles) chosen by server administrators.
- **Activity & Engagement Metrics**:
  - Message counters and timestamp metadata for Leveling/XP progression and invite counters (tracking invite codes and uses).
- **Transient Audio & Command Inputs**:
  - Search queries for music playback and voice channel IDs for Lavalink audio connections (not permanently stored).
- **AI Processing Inputs**:
  - User prompts submitted to AI commands are processed transiently via the Google Gemini API to return responses and are not stored permanently by Zyro.
- **Moderation & Audit Records**:
  - Moderation actions (warns, mutes, kicks, bans) and ticket transcripts are stored strictly for the designated server's administrative records.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Deliver requested bot services (music playback, ticketing, moderation, leveling, welcome cards).
- Maintain guild-specific settings and permissions.
- Protect servers from raids, spam, and malicious behavior through Zyro Shield.
- Prevent abuse, rate-limit violations, and technical failures.

**We DO NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user or server data to third parties.**

---

### 3. Third-Party Service Providers
Zyro integrates with trusted third-party providers solely to provide core bot functionality:
- **Discord API**: To receive events, execute commands, and send messages in accordance with Discord Developer Policies.
- **Lavalink v4 Audio Nodes**: To process and stream high-fidelity audio tracks.
- **Google Gemini API**: To process natural language understanding for AI features in accordance with Google's API Privacy standards.

---

### 4. Data Storage, Security & Retention
- **Security**: Data is stored securely with restricted access and protected by industry-standard encryption protocols.
- **Retention**: Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide bot services to your server. If Zyro is removed from a server, guild-specific configuration data can be deleted upon request.
- **No Message Scraping**: Zyro does not record, harvest, or store general private message histories across servers.

---

### 5. User Rights & Data Deletion
Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA) and Discord Developer Policies, you have the right to:
- Request an export of your stored personal data associated with your Discord User ID.
- Request the complete deletion of your data (e.g., XP, level records, moderation history).

To request data deletion, join the **Zyro Support Server** or contact the bot administration team. Valid deletion requests are processed within 14 business days.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Zyro complies with Discord's Terms of Service and does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or under the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced in our support server.
